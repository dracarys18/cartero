Cartero is a news reader for the Cartero feed, a hand-picked stream of stories on AI, systems, security and more. It also works with any RSS, Atom or JSON Feed.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>A clean reader with full articles, proper typography, images, code blocks and tables</li>
<li>Filter by topic and source, or show only what you haven't read</li>
<li>Rules that notify you or save stories automatically by topic, source or keyword</li>
<li>Saved stories are downloaded for offline reading</li>
<li>RSS 2.0, Atom and JSON Feed, with OPML import and export. Paste a site address and the feed is found for you</li>
<li>Swipe right to save, swipe left to mark as read</li>
<li>Material You dark theme, and a side-by-side list and reader on tablets</li>
</ul>

<b>Private by design</b>
No accounts, no ads and no analytics. Feeds are fetched straight from your device, and background sync runs on the schedule you choose.
