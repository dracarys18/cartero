Cartero (Spanish for "postman") is a content aggregator written in Go. I used to follow HackerNews and Lobsters a lot, and I have a huge list of curated blogs, but people flood HackerNews with random, low-quality posts. Cartero sits between you and those articles like middleware: it picks the ones that match your preferences and delivers only the good-quality ones to you.
